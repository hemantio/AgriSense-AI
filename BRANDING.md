# AgriSense AI — Brand Identity & Corporate Logo Guidelines

This document outlines the corporate identity guidelines for AgriSense AI. It defines the logo design, color typography, and correct usage parameters for digital dashboards, corporate reports, and mobile application interfaces.

---

### Design Concept
- **The Living Leaf:** Symbolizes sustainable farming, organic crops, and natural ecosystems.
- **The Neural Net Veins:** Represents AI-powered decision support, data-driven farming, and connected IoT telemetry.

## 2. Brand Color Palette (The Agrarian Codex)

The primary color codes are designed to convey dependability, precision, and nature:

| Color | Hex Code | Usage | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Living Emerald** | `#10B981` | Accent / Primary Brand Color | Represents crop health, active states |
| **Absolute Soil** | `#030303` | Main Background (Dark Mode) | Grounding element, high contrast |
| **Deep Forest** | `#064E3B` | Brand Secondary / Deep Green | Borders, headers, card backgrounds |
| **Agrarian Gold** | `#F59E0B` | Secondary Accent / Highlights | Warning states, high-value highlights |
| **Orchid** | `#FFB1EE` | Dynamic Highlight | Active selections, special metrics |

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

To maintain the high-precision aesthetic, use a clean sans-serif typeface alongside monospace for telemetry data:

- **Headings & Titles:** *Inter* or *Outfit* (Font-weight: 700+ / Black)
- **Body Text:** *Inter* or *Roboto* (Font-weight: 400 / Regular)
- **Telemetry / Code / Data:** *JetBrains Mono* or *Fira Code* (Font-weight: 500 / Medium)

---

## 4. Logo Application Guidelines

- **On Light Backgrounds:** Use the transparent vector variant with dark forest green lines.
- **On Dark Dashboards (Preferred):** Use the glowing emerald variant (as shown above) to stand out against high-contrast black backgrounds.
- **Minimum Safe Area:** Maintain a margin of at least 25% of the logo's width on all sides to prevent visual clutter.
